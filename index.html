Academic & Research Work

Deep Research.
Real Analysis.

Strategic projects, case studies and reports developed during MBA (Business Analytics) at Marwadi University.

MBA Research · PPT

Bridging the Premium Paradox — Kellanova India Strategy

45-day deep-dive across ~7,650 retail touchpoints diagnosing Kellanova's market bottlenecks in India. Developed a tiered value proposition, GPS-enabled SFA roadmap, and glocalization strategy.

BBA Case Study · PPT

India's Soft Power Gap — Cinema, Arts & Tourism vs Japan, Korea, Hollywood

Strategic comparative analysis benchmarking India's cultural assets against global leaders. Proposed a 3-pillar approach: centralized cultural agency, infrastructure integration, and FDI reform.

MBA Marketing · Report

Marketing Management Analysis — Nothing Technologies

SWOT, competitor benchmarking vs Apple, Samsung & Xiaomi, and a 200+ respondent survey revealing 52.7% purchase intent driven by design-led differentiation.

BBA Finance · Report

PNB Scam — Financial Fraud & Corporate Governance Failure

In-depth forensic case study on Nirav Modi's $1.8B LoU fraud. Analyzed internal collusion, SWIFT vulnerabilities, and proposed post-scandal governance reforms.

MBA Strategy · Report

Byju's — Blue Ocean Strategy to Corporate Governance Collapse

Applied Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, and BCG Matrix to chart Byju's rise and fall. Key finding: valuable AI assets undermined by absent governance and culture.

Research · Toei Animation

Toei Animation — Structure, Strategy & Industry Impact

Full SWOT and strategic analysis of Toei Animation (Dragon Ball, Sailor Moon). Explored production innovation, CGI integration, HR practices and market challenges for a personal passion project.
